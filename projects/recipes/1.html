French Toast

"Quote"-Family Member

Whether it was a holiday or Saturday morning, French Toast was always one of my favorite things when I was younger. Because of that, I've decided to choose it for my project to help spread this delicious breakfast meal.


Yield

This recipe makes 12 servings. It takes about 10 minutes to prep and about 20 to cook.

Tips & Tricks

If you've never made this before and aren't sure about what you should and shouldn't do, I've got you covered! :)

  • The best way to serve french toast is with maple syrup, powdered sugar and fruit. If you want somthing a little more savory, bacon or sausage is the way to go!

  • Because you should cook one or two at a time, putting your finished pieces (while you're cooking the rest) in the oven at about 200 F keeps them warm and crispy.

  • If you have extra eggs, adding an extra yolk will make it much richer and gives it a silkier texture.

  • If you want some extra fanciness, add some sugar before you put it on the pan the brulee the toast.

Ingredients

This recipe only needs 8 things, which are all probably already in your kitchen.

  • Flour: 1/4 cup. This is what makes it so fluffy!
  • Milk: 1 cup. Can be substituted with cream, half and half or another milk alternative.
  • Eggs: 3 large eggs. This gives it a nice, rich texture!
  • Sugar: 1tbsp of white sugar. I'm sure you know what this adds.
  • Vanilla: 1 tsp of extract. Adds flavor, one of the most magical things in baking.
  • Cinnamon: 1/2 tsp. Adds some more flavor!
  • Salt: 1 pinch of salt.
  • Bread: The recipe calls for 12 slices, but use as many as you want to make. Should be about an inch thick for ideal fluffiness. You can use any bread in your cupboard but I strongly recommend using brioche.

Cooking Instructions

Now we need to actually make it. Here are the simple steps:

  1. Put your flour into a large mixing bowl. Whisk in your milk until smooth.
  2. Whisk in your eggs, sugar, vanilla, cinnamon and salt until well combined.
  3. Lightly oil a frying pan over medium heat.
  4. Soak your bread slices in the milk mixture until saturated.
  5. Cook your bread slices on the pan until golden brown on both sides, which is about 2 minutes. Do one or two at a time.

And that's it! Serve it with fruit, syrup, powdered sugar or even bacon and enjoy!
