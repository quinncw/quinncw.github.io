Pumpkin Pie

"Quote"-Family Member

I chose this recipe because I think Pumpkin Pie is not only delicious but pretty simple. Here are the ingredients and instructions:

Yield

This recipe makes a 9-inch pie that is about 8 servings. It takes about 10 to prep and 50 to cook.

Tips & Tricks

Baking can be kind of annoying some times. So I've thought of 3 tips and tricks to make it a little easier.

  • If you want to know when the Pumpkin Pie has fully cooked, simply poke the center about an inch or two deep with either a knife or a toothpick. If it comes out clean, the Pumpkin Pie is cooked!

  • If you aren't a huge pumpkin fan or want to add a little extra sweetness, I recommend adding a dolop of whipped cream to your slice. It makes it a little less pumpkiny and in my opinion, a lot better.

  • One of the most important things to note is that pumpkin pie should always be refrigerated because it includes eggs. Make sure to keep it refrigerated!

    • Ingredients

    This recipe only includes 5 ingredients! See what I mean about it being simple?

    For this recipe, you'll need:

    • Pie Crust: 9-inch crust. This can easily be bought at a store, but feel free to make your own! But you'll have to find a recipe for it yourself.

    • Pumpkin Puree: You'll need 16oz. Again, this can be store bought or you can puree it yourself.

    • Sweetended Condensed Milk: 14oz can. A can of this will make it much richer.

    • Eggs: Two whole eggs.

    • Pumpkin Pie Spice: 1tsp. This isn't necessary but strongly recommended for flavor. Can be store bought or made yourself with a blend of spices.

    Cooking Instructions

    Now that you've gathered your ingredients, we need to start making the pie.

    1. Preheat your oven to 425 degrees F/220 degrees C.
    2. Put your pie crust into a pie dish and place it on a baking sheet.
    3. Add your pumpkin puree, condensed milk, eggs, and the pumpkin spice in a large bowl. Mix well.
    4. Pour the mixture into the crust and bake for 15 minutes in the oven. Reduce the heat to 350 degrees F/175 degrees C and continue to bake for 35-40 minutes or until filling is set.
    5. Remove from oven and cool on a wire rack.

    That was it! The last thing to do is enjoy your pumpkin pie. :)
